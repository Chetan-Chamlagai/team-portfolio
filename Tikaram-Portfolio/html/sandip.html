About Me

I'm a Nepali tech entrepreneur, software engineer and educator based in Damak, Jhapa. At Tufan Transport I lead the technology behind our subscription-based ride-sharing platform, and I'm the Founder & CEO of A1 IT Innovation — a full-service IT company delivering web development, mobile apps, DevOps managed services, AI solutions and online tech education across Nepal.

I hold a Master of Information Technology (MIT) and a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA), both from Tribhuvan University. Through free IT internships and college workshops, my team has trained 500+ students with real-world project experience.
